# geoscales: Nested Regions and Spatial Hierarchies for Modeling

Discrete spatial representations for optimization and simulation models,
including nested sets of regions, conversion between alternative spatial
resolutions, and mapping of data onto region hierarchies for modeling
and visualization.

## Details

Spatial companion to the
[timescales](https://github.com/optimal2050/timescales) package.
